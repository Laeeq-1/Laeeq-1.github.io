EVCIM

Creating the Foundation

We started by recovering recycled metal from abandoned mattresses. The springs were heated to straighten them and then cut using a plier. This metal was mixed with concrete to create our homemade weight plates.

Painting the Weights

Once the concrete dried, the plates were coated in matte black paint. At this stage, we finalized the 5kg weight plate design. However, we could only produce six at a time.

Our Finished Product

The result: clean, consistent 5kg concrete weights reinforced with metal. A major milestone, but we weren’t done yet.

Experimenting with Moulds

To scale up, we began developing a more consistent mould using recycled plastics. The aim was to perfect a system that would always produce identical weights at a more frequent rate.

Drying Rack Innovation

We repurposed discarded wooden planks to build a drying rack. This allowed us to cure multiple weights simultaneously and improve our workflow.

Reflection

At the core of the EVCIM project is a belief that fitness should be accessible to everyone. Gym equipment, especially weights, can be expensive — but through recycling, creativity, and sheer trial-and-error, we’ve managed to design affordable, functional weights from materials that would’ve ended up in landfills.

We’re not just making plates — we’re building a movement where passion meets sustainability. And we’re not stopping until everyone can train, lift, and grow without breaking the bank. If you want to check out our Instagram, click the icon below.
